# SOTG World Cup 2026 Predictor 🏆

A prediction game built for the SOTG group to battle it out across every match of the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Call every scoreline, rack up points, and see who really knows their football.

---

## How it works

You predict the exact scoreline for every match before it kicks off. The game scores you automatically once results are in:

| Result | Points |
|---|---|
| Exact scoreline ⚡ | 3 pts |
| Correct match result (W/D/L) ✅ | 1 pt |
| Wrong | 0 pts |

Predictions lock 15 minutes before kick-off — no last-second changes when the team sheets are out.

---

## Features

### 🎯 Match predictions
Predict every game from the Group Stage all the way through to the Final. The fixture view organises games by matchday, day, and stage so you're never scrolling past games you've already locked in.

### ⭐ Banker pick
Once per matchday you can star one prediction as your **Banker**. If your banker is correct, your points for that game double. Pick wisely — it only works on one match per matchday and you can switch it right up until the whistle.

### 🏆 Awards & specials
Beyond match scores, you can predict the big tournament outcomes: Winner, Runners-up, Player of the Tournament, Top Scorer, best confederation, SOTG specials like Team of the Tournament, and more. Each category has two picks — first choice earns more, second choice earns a consolation.

### 📊 Live leaderboard
The leaderboard updates as results come in. Points are computed automatically from a live database view so the standings are always accurate — no manual entry, no trust-me-bro spreadsheets.

### 👥 Community picks
Once a game kicks off, you can see how everyone else predicted it. A bar chart shows the most popular scorelines and how the community split — and you'll see a "👥 X others also picked 1-0" note if your prediction matches the crowd.

### 🏅 Achievements
Earn badges on your profile for hitting milestones: your first exact score, hat-tricks of exacts, prediction streaks, climbing to #1, and more. They're calculated from your actual stats — no grinding required, just predicting well.

### 🔴 Live score updates
When matches are in progress the predict page auto-refreshes every 30 seconds so you can watch your points change in real time without manually reloading.

### 🔒 Strictly fair
Predictions are locked in the **database** at kick-off, not just hidden in the UI. There's no way to backdate a pick, and you can't see anyone else's prediction for a game until it's kicked off. The leaderboard never stores a running total — it's always recalculated fresh from results, so a corrected score fixes everyone's points automatically on the next update.

## Your profile

Your `/me` page shows:
- Total points, exact scores, correct results, hit rate, and rank
- Current and longest prediction streaks
- Your achievement badge collection
- A shareable stats card you can fire into the group chat

## Leagues

Create a private league with your mates, share the invite code, and run a side table alongside the main leaderboard. The creator can rename or share the league; anyone can leave. Share the invite link directly from the league page and it'll drop new members straight into the right table after they sign up.

A post-tournament opinion poll unlocks at the end. Best team, worst team, best player, worst player — bragging rights for whoever called it.
